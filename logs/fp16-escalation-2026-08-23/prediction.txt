Pre-registered 2026-08-23, before running.

At an isolated batch-start zero the operands are intact: the weight sums to the
same value as at a clean call and the activations are healthy. Good input, empty
output. That was measured at one chunk where only isolated zeros occur.

Once a batch escalates and every call in it returns zero, the same question has
not been asked. Two readings:

  A. The operands stay intact through the cascade. Then escalation is more of the
     same failure, applied to every call rather than one, and the defect is
     wholly on the output side.

  B. The operands go bad during the cascade. Then the first zeroed call corrupts
     something that later calls read, and the escalation is a propagation. That
     would make the isolated zero the primary event and the cascade its
     consequence, which is a different shape of defect.

The discriminator is the activation sum at calls inside a cascade compared with
the same tensor at a clean call. Weight sums should be unchanged either way,
since weights are not written by inference.

The instrument must be shown able to report both outcomes: BC250_DEVCHECK
reports non-zero sums at clean calls and at isolated zeroed calls already, so a
zero or wild reading inside a cascade would be a genuine signal rather than the
probe failing.
