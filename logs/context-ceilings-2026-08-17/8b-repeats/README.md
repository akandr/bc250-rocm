model is much noisier than anything else measured here and the write-up quotes
ranges rather than single values for it.

`q8b_d16128.log` and `q8b_d7936.log` are the two depths. The ten-run series that
the coefficient-of-variation figures come from is in
`../../loose-ends-2026-08-18/variance/`, and the graph-capture arms that
complicate the interpretation are in `../../loose-ends-2026-08-18/graphflag/`.
