Everything about the batch-boundary model comes from one model, qwen3-8B. A
pattern seen on one model is one model's pattern until shown otherwise.

qwen3-14B is the other model this defect is documented to affect. It has a
different layer count, so the calls per batch should differ from the 8B's 36, but
the structure should not: on the exact baseline (GGML_CUDA_NO_POOL=1, one chunk,
ubatch 512, four batches) the prediction is three zeros, one at the first fp16
GEMM of each batch after the first, evenly spaced by whatever the per-batch call
count turns out to be.

If instead the 14B shows no zeros, a different count, or zeros at positions that
are not batch starts, the model is 8B-specific and the generalisation in the
write-up is too broad.

Also worth knowing: qwen2.5-1.5B is recorded here as never reaching rocBLAS at
all on the Q4_K path, so a run showing zero GEMM calls on it would confirm the
instrument sees nothing rather than that the model is clean. That distinction has
caught this work out before.
