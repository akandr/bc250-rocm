# What the f32 compute-type workaround costs, 2026-08-21

Generated by `scripts/f32_workaround_cost.sh`.

## Why this was needed

The write-up says `GGML_CUDA_CUBLAS_COMPUTE_TYPE=f32` "costs nothing measurable
here". That is load-bearing: it is the recommended fix for the fp16 defect and
it appears in the README recipe. The four figures behind the sentence, prefill
238.7 against 238.9 and decode 32.31 plus or minus 3.01 against 32.28 plus or
minus 3.16 over five repetitions, survive in no shipped log, and the harness
that produced them is not among the shipped scripts, so the configuration cannot
be recovered. Rather than guess it, which is the mistake that made an earlier
re-measurement compare nothing, this measures the claim directly with the
configuration stated.

A first attempt on 2026-08-20 was void through an error in setting it up: two copies of the
script were launched and ran concurrently, interleaving into one log and
competing for the GPU. Most of its cells read FAIL and none of it is usable.
The harness now takes a lock so that cannot recur.

## Result: the claim holds

qwen3-8B Q8_0, mmap, flash attention on, pp512 and tg64, eight rounds with the
arms alternated ABBA:

| arm | pp512 mean | sd | tg64 mean | sd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| compute type f32 | 233.67 | 6.12 | 34.58 | 0.48 |
| default | 234.51 | 1.79 | 34.50 | 0.82 |

Prefill differs by 0.36 percent in favour of the default, decode by 0.22 percent
in favour of f32. Welch t is 0.37 and 0.23 respectively. Neither is a difference.

The f32 arm's prefill spread is inflated by its first run, 218.80 against 234 to
237 for every other run in both arms. Dropping that one cold-start reading gives
235.80 with sd 1.22 against the default's 234.51 with sd 1.79, which does not
change the conclusion and is reported rather than applied, since removing an
inconvenient first point without a stated rule is how results get massaged.

## What replaces the old figures

The four unsupported numbers should be dropped rather than repeated. They were
measured on a configuration that cannot be identified, and the ones above are
measured on a stated one. The claim they were supporting survives either way:
the workaround is free, and the reason to prefer it over the patch is
convenience rather than performance.
