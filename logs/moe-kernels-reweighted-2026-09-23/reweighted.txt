why this is not weighted by occurrence
  ROCm issues 437.7 matvec dispatches a token (rocprof trace), Vulkan 511 (perf logger).
  ROCm fuses pairs Vulkan runs separately: the iq2_xxs expert gate/up is 39.4 dispatches a
  token at 41.4 us on ROCm against 80 at 19.3 us on Vulkan, and the ROCm kernel is doing more
  than the Vulkan one (has_fusion folds the GLU in, which Vulkan dispatches 80 times at
  2.96 us). Per-shape counts are not shared between the backends and the dispatches are not
  even like for like, so a cross-backend weighted total is not defined for this graph.
