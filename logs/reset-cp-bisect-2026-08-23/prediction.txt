Pre-registered 2026-08-23, before running.

gfx_v10_0_cp_resume() is entered during reset recovery and never returns. Its
sequence, from the source, is:

  1. cp_gfx_load_microcode / cp_compute_load_microcode  (FW_LOAD_DIRECT only)
  2. gfx_v10_0_kiq_resume        the Kernel Interface Queue
  3. gfx_v10_0_kcq_resume        the Kernel Compute Queues
  4. cp_gfx_resume / cp_async_gfx_ring_resume
  5. amdgpu_ring_test_helper on each graphics ring

Prediction: the hang is in step 2 or 3, the KIQ or KCQ resume, rather than in the
graphics ring path.

The reason to expect it there is this board's own history. Its correctness defect
was a PASID flush issued through the KIQ, fixed by bc250_flush_pasid_kiq=0. Its
compute queues are the ones Mesa disables on gfx1013. Its preemption timeouts are
on compute queues. If a reset leaves one part of the CP unable to come back, the
compute side is where this hardware has always been weakest.

If instead the hang is in step 4 or 5, the graphics ring path, that is a newer
statement and would not connect to any existing defect here.

If it is in step 1, microcode loading, that is different again and would point at
firmware rather than queues.

Being wrong is informative in all three directions, which is why the prediction
is worth writing down.
