# Benchmarks on the default Fedora 44 configuration, 2026-09-15

**Everything on this page except `clock-corrected/` was measured with the GPU clock oscillating between 1000
and 2000 MHz**, because the Fedora 44 upgrade had replaced the oberon governor configuration with the package
default. Treat these numbers as a record of that state, not as figures for the machine. The corrected
campaign is in `clock-corrected/` and is what the front page uses.

Generated by [`scripts/campaign_f44.sh`](../../scripts/campaign_f44.sh) (`campaign/`), plus the follow-up
runs below, all in one Fedora 44 boot. Configuration in `campaign/log`: Fedora 44, kernel
7.1.8-100.fc43 with the bc250 amdgpu module (`bc250_flush_by_runlist=3`, `bc250_flush_pasid_kiq=0`,
`bc250_cc_write_mode=3`, `gpu_recovery=0`), SELinux enforcing, `rocm-hip-7.1.1-3`, `rocm-comgr-20-13`,
`mesa-vulkan-drivers-26.1.8-1`. ROCm resolves `librocblas.so.5` and `libamd_comgr.so.3` from
`/opt/bc250-rocm/lib64` ([`scripts/install_rocm711_overrides.sh`](../../scripts/install_rocm711_overrides.sh)),
with no environment variables apart from `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=0`. llama.cpp 7ba604f with the three patches,
built for ROCm (`build-hip-f44`) and Vulkan (`build-vk-f44`) from the same tree.

## Decode stability at depth 0 (`vulkan-modes/`)

Ten consecutive invocations per backend, alternated, `-p 0 -n 64 -r 5`, qwen2.5-1.5B: Vulkan 240.53 to
242.03 and ROCm 144.38 to 147.38 across all 50 samples each. Vulkan's low readings at depth 0 above occur
only when other depths share the invocation.

## GPGPU (`gpgpu/`)

Probes from `patches/` rebuilt against ROCm 7.1.1 (`clang++ -x hip --offload-arch=gfx1013 -O2 ... -lrocblas
-lamdhip64`):

- SGEMM, 21 iterations, all correct, median of iterations 2 to 21: N=512 0.2 ms, 1024 0.8, 2048 5.7, 4096
  23.2, 8192 187.55. The probe prints milliseconds to one decimal.
- DGEMM N=2048 (the probe's default, as on Fedora 43): 605.7 GFLOP/s on iterations 8 and 9, all correct;
  N=4096: 607.2.
- Streaming-read bandwidth, 2048 MiB buffer, three runs: 433.9, 433.8, 433.5 GB/s.

## llama.cpp build variants (`variants/`)

[`scripts/llamacpp_variants_f44.sh`](../../scripts/llamacpp_variants_f44.sh), one invocation per model with
`-p 512 -n 64 -r 3`, so decode on the 14B carries the artefact above:

| build | 1.5B pp / tg | 8B pp / tg | qwen3-14B pp / tg | MoE pp / tg | 1.5B gate | 8B gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7ba604f + 3 patches | 993.71 / 146.69 | 306.30 / 37.85 | 125.81 / 26.04 | 369.50 / 40.91 | 8.9442 | 9.1117 |
| same, `-DGGML_CUDA_FORCE_CUBLAS=ON` | 1033.35 / 146.59 | 175.02 / 39.24 | 124.47 / 17.09 | 219.79 / 40.50 | 8.9479 | 9.1450 |
| master bfdc321 + 0002, 0003 | 991.28 / 147.92 | 303.26 / 39.36 | 124.90 / 21.39 | 367.21 / 41.37 | 8.9490 | 9.1158 |

The master bench rows were re-run with `--load-mode none`, since master removed `-mmp` and the first pass
recorded nothing. Forcing cuBLAS costs 43 percent of 8B prefill and 41 percent of MoE prefill and moves both
gates; it is not an improvement. Master's gates differ from 7ba604f's in the third decimal, as on Fedora 43.
qwen3-14B decode alone, `-p 0 -n 64 -r 3`, four alternated pairs (`q14tg_*.jsonl`): 7ba604f 25.62 to 27.07,
master 27.34 to 28.69, about 6 percent faster on master.

## The same campaign at the corrected clock (`clock-corrected/`)

Re-run on 16 September after restoring the 1500 MHz governor configuration, same script and rounds, plus the
speed probes (`f44fixed_*`) and the GPGPU probes (`sgemm_*`, `dgemm_default.log`, `membw_*`). Medians of nine
samples ([`scripts/campaign_medians.py`](../../scripts/campaign_medians.py) reproduces this table from `clock-corrected/log`):

| qwen3-8B | 243.57 | 394.77 | 38.94 | 39.07 |

GPGPU: SGEMM N=4096 30.1 ms, N=8192 237.9 ms, DGEMM N=2048 456.2 GFLOP/s, streaming-read bandwidth 432.3,

system time.

Every figure is within 2 percent of the Fedora 43 campaign, in both directions.

## llama.cpp master against the measured base, at the corrected clock (`master-vs-base/`)

[`scripts/master_vs_base_f44.sh`](../../scripts/master_vs_base_f44.sh). The earlier variant comparison ran
while the governor was oscillating and suggested master decoded qwen3-14B about 6 percent faster; repeated
at 1500 MHz with one test per invocation, three alternated rounds of `-r 3`, that shrinks to under 2 percent.
Medians of nine samples:

| model | base pp512 | master pp512 | base tg64 | master tg64 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen2.5-1.5B | 792.18 | 785.94 | 117.56 | 116.79 |
| qwen3-8B | 243.68 | 241.68 | 39.02 | 38.95 |
| qwen3-14B | 96.83 | 96.03 | 21.81 | 22.16 |
| qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE | 289.82 | 286.60 | 34.24 | 34.79 |

Master (`bfdc321` with patches 0002 and 0003; 0001 is upstream) prefills 0.8 to 1.1 percent slower on every
model and decodes within 1 percent except the two larger models, where it is 1.6 percent faster. Gates:
master 8.9490 and 9.1158 against the base's 8.9442 and 9.1117, the third-decimal difference already recorded
in [`../llamacpp-master-recheck-2026-09-14/`](../llamacpp-master-recheck-2026-09-14/). Nothing here argues for
moving off the measured base.
