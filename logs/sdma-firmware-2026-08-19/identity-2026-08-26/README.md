# What the two SDMA blobs actually are, 2026-08-26

Generated by
[`scripts/sdma_firmware_identity.sh`](../../../scripts/sdma_firmware_identity.sh) on kernel 7.1.8.
`identity.txt` is the run.

The parent page said the board's `cyan_skillfish2` SDMA microcode and the navi12 blob that replaces
it are "the same SDMA 5.0 format, differing only in the ucode version field (0x34 against 0x2c)",
from a reading that was not kept. Two of those three claims hold and the third does not.

## What holds

Both are SDMA 5.0 and both are 33792 bytes. The header reads `ip_version` major 5, minor 0 on each,
and the version fields are exactly as quoted: the board's original is `ucode_version` **0x34** and
navi12 is **0x2c**.

Reading the original at all takes a detour worth recording. The substitution overwrites
`cyan_skillfish2_sdma.bin.xz` with navi12's bytes, so on a board carrying the fix the two paths hold
the same file and comparing them shows nothing: identical compressed md5
`58355d1f7857a3737d5f85c5f8aadb42` and identical decompressed
`3febefa5396a646fee8f1bd4f453062a`. That identity is the check that the substitution is in place,
and it is the only thing the installed files can tell you. The original survives only in the backup
the recipe tells you to take, `~/usr/lib/firmware/amdgpu/`, which is where the 0x34 above comes
from.

## What does not hold

"Differing only in the ucode version field" is wrong, and not by a little. Comparing the decompressed
blobs byte by byte, **17988 of 33792 bytes differ**, which is 53 percent of the file.

The differences fall in two places. In the header, only `ucode_version` (offset 17, 0x34 against
0x2c) and `crc32` (offsets 29 to 32) change, which is what the original claim was presumably
reading. Everything else in the header matches, including size and IP version. But from offset 257
onward, which is where `ucode_array_offset_bytes` says the microcode itself begins, the two payloads
are substantially different code.

So this is not one microcode with a version stamp changed. It is a different SDMA program of the
same generation and size. That matters for how the fix reads: the board is not being talked out of a
version check, it is being given microcode that works where its own does not, and nothing here says
why the shipped `cyan_skillfish2` build fails. The parent page's conclusion, that the defect was the
firmware rather than the board, is unaffected and if anything is better supported by this.
