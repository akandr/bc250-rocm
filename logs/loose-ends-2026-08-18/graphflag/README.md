What `GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1` costs, measured because it had been used as a
control without ever being characterised.

Ten runs of the 8B at a primed depth of 16128 in each arm, one boot:

| arm | mean t/s | sd | coefficient of variation | range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| graph capture disabled | 15.19 | 2.45 | 16.1 percent | 10.03 to 19.18 |
| graph capture enabled | 17.80 | 1.16 | 6.5 percent | 15.87 to 19.37 |

So the flag costs about 15 percent of throughput and multiplies the spread by
two and a half. That matters beyond the flag itself: a decode-variance
comparison across three models had used it on one model and not the others,
which makes that comparison unusable. See the README.

At depth 0 the same contrast nearly disappears (23.4 to 26.4 with the flag over
six runs against 23.5 to 24.3 without over three), so this is specific to
decoding with a large cache resident.

`sigbus-backtrace.txt` is a separate matter found in the same round: one run in
roughly twelve died with SIGBUS inside `rocr::AMD::AqlQueue::StoreRelaxed`,
reached from `hipStreamQuery` through `submitMarker` and `dispatchBarrierPacket`,
with no GPU fault logged. It did not recur in the ten runs of the first arm here.
