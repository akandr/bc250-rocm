Pre-registered before any arm was run, cycle 16, 2026-08-24.

Question. A delay before the KIQ initialisation does nothing. Prints placed
between the scheduler register read and its matching write let the sequence run
six steps further. Those prints are delay and bus activity at once, so which of
the two is doing the work is unknown. This build varies only what sits between
the two accesses.

Arms, all in one module, selected at runtime through
/sys/module/amdgpu/parameters/bc250_kiq_gap_mode:

  0  nothing between the accesses
  1  msleep(50), delay with scheduling, no device traffic
  2  udelay busy-wait for 50 ms, delay without scheduling
  3  dev_warn, the known-helpful case, positive control

Ruler. Six step markers inside gfx_v10_0_kiq_init_queue() report how far the
reset path gets. Nothing tried so far has made the board survive a reset, so
"helped" means "got further", never "worked".

Predictions.

  Arm 0 stalls inside gfx_v10_0_kiq_setting(), printing no step markers. This
  reproduces the coarse baseline from 23 August and is what makes the rest
  readable.

  Arm 3 prints step markers, reaching at least step 5, reproducing the earlier
  finer run. If it does not, the effect is not stable across builds and no
  conclusion can be drawn from the other arms.

  Arms 1 and 2 stall like arm 0, printing no step markers. This follows from the
  settle-delay falsification: if 500 ms before the function does nothing, 50 ms
  of the same kind of waiting a few instructions later should also do nothing.

What each outcome would mean.

  Arms 1 and 2 fail, arm 3 works: delay is not the mechanism and the printk path
  itself is, which points at bus or interconnect activity between two register
  accesses rather than at a settling time.

  Arm 1 works and arm 2 fails: the scheduling is the mechanism, not the delay,
  since the two differ in little else.

  Arms 1 and 2 both work: position matters and duration matters, and the
  settle-delay result would mean only that the delay was in the wrong place.

  Arm 3 fails: the whole line of reasoning rests on an unstable observation and
  has to be abandoned rather than explained.
