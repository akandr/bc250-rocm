Pre-registered 2026-08-24, before running.

The stall inside the KIQ initialisation moves when prints are added: with fewer
prints it stops inside gfx_v10_0_kiq_setting(), with more it runs six steps
further. The only difference is the delay each print costs. That reads as a
device needing time to settle after a reset.

If that reading is right, an explicit delay at the top of the reset path in
gfx_v10_0_kiq_init_queue() should let the sequence complete and the board should
survive a deliberate GPU reset.

Prediction: with msleep(50) there, the board survives, the KIQ probes run to
completion, and the resume finishes.

Three outcomes are possible and all are worth having:
  - The board survives and is usable afterwards. That is a fix, the first this
    defect has had, and the delay threshold then becomes worth finding.
  - The board survives but the GPU is unusable afterwards. That is not a fix; it
    would mean the reset completes without restoring a working device, and
    "alive" must not be reported as "working".
  - The board still dies. That falsifies the settle-time reading, and the timing
    sensitivity would need another explanation.

The third outcome is the one to guard against overreading. A single surviving run
would not be enough either; if it survives, repeat it.
