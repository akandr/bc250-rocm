# Do the documented defects still behave as documented, 2026-08-22

Generated by `scripts/defects_still_open.sh`, with the fault hunt stopped.
`state.txt` records the configuration.

## Why

A defect listed as open that has quietly stopped happening is as wrong as a
defect that was missed. Three things changed under this board after the open
defects were characterised: the navi12 SDMA microcode, kernel 7.1.8, and
`amdgpu.gpu_recovery=0`. Nothing had re-checked them as a set.

## The fp16 defect is still open

Three rounds on the 8B, alternating compute type, context 2048 over two chunks:

| round | `COMPUTE_TYPE=f32` | `COMPUTE_TYPE=f16` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9.0975 | 17.1068 |
| 2 | 9.0975 | 14.7398 |
| 3 | 9.0975 | 21.4429 |

The workaround arm is bit-identical three times over; the defect arm is wrong
every time and by a different amount each time. Both halves of the entry in the
defect table still hold, including the non-determinism.

## The SDMA fix still holds

With `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1` the byte-boundary probe reports `ALL SIZES COMPLETED`.
The microcode substitution has survived the kernel change.

## SGEMM, and a measurement artifact worth stating

Twenty iterations per size against the front page figures:

| N | published GFLOP/s | average over 20 | warm-only estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 512 | about 1340 | 542 | 1645 |
| 1024 | about 2680 | 1851 | 2488 |
| 2048 | about 3010 | 3019 | 3051 |
| 4096 | about 4580 | 4543 | 4365 |
| 8192 | about 4660 | 4643 | 4418 |

The three large sizes reproduce closely. The two small ones look far below the
published figures, and that is this harness rather than the board. A single
iteration at N=512 takes 6.8 ms against about 0.16 ms warm, so one cold call
costs more than the other nineteen together and drags the average down. The
published table quotes a median per GEMM, which is insensitive to that. Removing
one cold call of the measured size brings N=512 to about 1645 and N=1024 to about
2488, either side of the published values.

So nothing here contradicts the front page, but the comparison as first run
would have suggested a 60 percent regression at N=512 that does not exist. The
figures are reported both ways rather than only the flattering one.

No GPU faults, preemption failures or resets during the run.
