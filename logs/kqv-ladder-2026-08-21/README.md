# The KQV context ladder, reproduced, 2026-08-21

Generated by `scripts/kqv_ladder_remeasure.sh`.

## Why

An earlier write-up of the KQV precision patch carried a table showing the
defect scaling with context: correct at 1024, about 174 at 2048, and 180 to 3764
across runs at 4096. A figure audit found no shipped log behind the 1024 value
or the 4096 upper bound, and the earlier re-measurement had covered ctx 4096
only, so the ladder itself had never been reproduced. Configuration copied from
`scripts/kqv_removal_remeasure.sh` rather than guessed, since that harness pins
what the original figures came from: qwen2.5-1.5B Q4_K_M, chunks 2, `-fa off`,
`GGML_CUDA_NO_VMM=1`.

## Result

Three runs per rung without the patch, one with it restored:

| context | without the patch | with the patch |
|---|---|---|
| 1024 | 8.1616, 8.1616, 708.4671 | 8.1702 |
| 2048 | 10.0103, 10.0103, 1347.6541 | 10.0204 |
| 4096 | 224.8379, 211.8500, 8234.2048 | 11.0631 |

The patch works, and the ctx 4096 patched value reproduces the earlier
re-measurement to four decimals, 11.0631 against 11.0631.

## Two corrections to the earlier figures

**The defect is not absent at short context.** The earlier table said ctx 1024 is
"correct, 8/8 bit-identical". Here two of three runs are correct and the third
returns 708.4671, which is not a small deviation. The same happens at 2048. What
changes with context is not whether the defect occurs but how badly and how
often, and a table row reading "correct" at 1024 states something stronger than
the data supports.

**The quoted values do not reproduce.** The earlier 9.8260 at ctx 1024 comes out
as 8.1616 here, and the 4096 range of 180 to 3764 comes out as 211 to 8234. The
patched arm reproduces exactly while the unpatched arm does not, which is what
one would expect of a corrupting defect whose magnitude varies, but it means the
earlier specific numbers belong to a configuration that cannot now be
identified. They should be replaced by these rather than defended.

## An observation that was tested and did not hold

The catastrophic run was the third one at every rung, positions 3, 6 and 9 of
nine, noted here as about a 0.3 percent coincidence if runs failed
independently, and flagged as possibly periodic. Twelve consecutive identical
runs at one context settled it: no period, nine of twelve wrong at scattered
positions ([`../kqv-periodicity-2026-08-21/`](../kqv-periodicity-2026-08-21/)).
The pattern here was chance.

That run also shows the rate above is unreliable. Three samples per rung gave two
correct in three at context 2048; twelve samples give three correct in twelve.
Pooled, five in fifteen are correct at that context, so the defect strikes about
two runs in three. Read the table above as showing that the defect reaches every
context tested, which is the part that holds, and not as an estimate of how often.

Note that at ctx 4096 all three runs are wrong, 224.8 and 211.9 as well as
8234.2, against a correct 11.06. So "correct then catastrophic" describes the
two shorter rungs only, and even there it is not the pattern it looked like.
